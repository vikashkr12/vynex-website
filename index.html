What we build

Every project starts with how your team works today. We build what fits, connect it to the tools you already use, and keep it running after launch.

Automations

Software that does the repetitive work: reading invoices, moving data between systems, sending reports on time.

Built with UiPath, Python, REST APIs, Excel and VBA, Google Apps Script

  • Invoice, order and document processing
  • Data entry between tools, without copy-paste
  • Scheduled reports and email alerts
  • Connecting the apps you already pay for

Web apps

Internal tools and customer-facing apps, built around how your team actually works.

Built with JavaScript, Supabase, REST APIs, Vercel

  • Order, inventory and job trackers
  • Client portals and booking flows
  • Admin panels for your business data
  • Replacing the spreadsheet that became the system

Dashboards

Your numbers in one place, updated on their own. No more asking someone to send the latest file.

Built with Supabase, Google Sheets, Excel, Apps Script

  • Sales, expense and cash-flow views
  • Live data from sheets, CRMs and databases
  • Exports ready to share with your CA
  • Daily or weekly summaries by email

How we work

A written scope before any work starts, working versions early, and support once it's live.

  1. Understand

    You walk us through how the work happens today: the tools, the people, and where time goes.

  2. Scope and quote

    You get a written scope, timeline and price before anything is built.

  3. Build
