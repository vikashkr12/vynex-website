Questions

Anything else? Email reachvikashkumar.mail@gmail.com.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A single automation is usually much quicker than a full web app. Your written quote includes a timeline before you pay anything.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses in the UAE and the US as well as India. Everything is done remotely, calls are scheduled to suit your time zone, and quotes and invoices can be in USD or AED, paid by international bank transfer.

How does payment work?

Once you approve the quote, you pay the full amount, online or by bank transfer, and work begins. If you cancel before work starts, you get a full refund. Details are in our refund and cancellation policy.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the code, files and accounts we set up for you are yours. We hand over everything with documentation.

What do you need from us to start?

Someone who knows the process, access to the tools involved, and a few real examples of the work, such as sample invoices or reports.

Will it work with the tools we already use?

Usually, yes. We connect to spreadsheets, email, accounting and business apps through their APIs or exports. We'll tell you during scoping if something isn't possible.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Fixes for problems in what we delivered are covered for an agreed period after handover, stated in your quote. For ongoing changes and monitoring, you can add monthly support.

Tell us what you do by hand today.

Describe the process or tool you have in mind. You'll get a reply with a few questions and a time to talk.
