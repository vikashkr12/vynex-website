What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email address, company name, phone number, the service you are interested in, and your message.
  • Emails and calls: whatever you choose to share with us.
  • Payments: online payments in India are processed by our payment gateway, and international payments are received through our bank or payment partners. We receive confirmation details such as your name, the amount, the currency and a transaction reference. We do not receive or store your full card details.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver and support your project, send invoices, keep accounting records, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run this website and our business:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers enquiry form submissions to our email
  • Google, for email and website fonts
  • Vercel, which hosts this website
  • Cashfree Payments, which processes online payments in India
  • Our bank and international payment partners, which receive payments from clients outside India

We may also disclose information when required by law.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Payment pages provided by Cashfree may use cookies needed to process your payment.

Data in client projects

When a project needs access to your systems or data, we access only what the project requires, keep it confidential, and remove our access on request after handover.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as Indian tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
